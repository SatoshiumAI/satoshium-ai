# Satoshium Connect

**Directory:** `/connect/`  
**Public page:** `index.html`  
**Status:** Active public connection and contact hub

## Purpose

The `/connect/` directory provides a central public-facing hub for reaching the principal channels associated with the Satoshium ecosystem.

Its public `index.html` gathers social, development, communication, coordination, and contact links so visitors can follow the public build, explore the broader Satoshium domain architecture, and reach the appropriate outward-facing platform.

## Repository Role

This directory documents and presents connection surfaces. It is not itself a formal Satoshium Suite institution, canonical operational object, governance layer, or authority-bearing record.

Its responsibility is navigational and communicative: helping visitors reach established Satoshium public channels from one consolidated location.

## Authority Boundary

The Connect page aggregates references to other Satoshium surfaces. Those references do not transfer the authority, provenance, institutional role, or canonical responsibility of the referenced destination into `/connect/`.

In particular:

- **Connection ≠ Identity.**
- **Reference ≠ Derivation.**
- **Reference ≠ Support.**
- **Reference ≠ Authority Transfer.**

**REFERENCE DOES NOT TRANSFER AUTHORITY.**

Formal Suite institutions and their canonical responsibilities remain separate from this directory.
