# 🧠 Intelligence Layer
## Uses Meaning

The Intelligence Layer is where reasoning, tools, workflows, and future agents operate within the boundaries defined by the rest of the Satoshium system.

It transforms trusted inputs and structured knowledge into:

- decisions
- interpretation
- adaptive behavior
- bounded automation

Intelligence in Satoshium is not treated as the foundation of the system. It is a capability built on top of stronger layers.

---

# Why the Intelligence Layer Matters

Intelligence is powerful, but without boundaries it can become opaque, inconsistent, or detached from reality.

The Intelligence Layer exists so that Satoshium can explore useful intelligence without surrendering:

- clarity
- structure
- verifiability
- shared meaning

> Intelligence is most useful when it operates inside trusted systems and shared meaning.

In Satoshium, intelligence is not a black box. It is a structured capability operating within defined inputs and system assumptions.

---

# What the Intelligence Layer Establishes

The Intelligence Layer introduces reasoning capabilities that operate inside the platform’s architectural constraints.

## Reasoning

Supports interpretation, planning, and decision-making using structured knowledge and trustworthy inputs.

## Tools and Workflows

Connects intelligence to useful actions through tools, workflows, and bounded automation systems.

## Agents

Future agents may exist within this layer as infrastructure components rather than as the platform’s central identity.

## Constraints

Keeps intelligent behavior aligned with:

- canonical terminology
- trust assumptions
- system goals
- architectural intent

---

# Intelligence Within the Satoshium Model

Satoshium does not treat intelligence as the starting point of the system.

Instead:

1. the Trust Layer verifies what can be relied upon
2. the Knowledge Layer defines what terms and structures mean
3. the Intelligence Layer uses those foundations to reason and act

This order preserves system coherence over time.

Intelligence without trusted foundations can drift into inconsistency. Intelligence grounded in structure remains interpretable and useful.
