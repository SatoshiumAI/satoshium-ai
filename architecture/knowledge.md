# 📚 Knowledge Layer
## Defines Meaning

The Knowledge Layer establishes the shared language of the Satoshium ecosystem.

It provides the canonical vocabulary, definitions, architectural concepts, and trust-model terminology that allow the broader platform to remain:

- coherent
- explainable
- interoperable
- durable over time

The Knowledge Layer ensures that Satoshium is not only built, but described clearly — to itself, to future builders, and to the public.

---

# Why the Knowledge Layer Matters

Systems cannot remain trustworthy, interoperable, or understandable without a stable internal language.

The Knowledge Layer exists so that coordination across the platform can occur through shared meaning rather than interpretation drift.

> Before systems can coordinate well, they must define meaning well.

In Satoshium, language is treated as infrastructure rather than decoration.

Definitions, specifications, and conceptual consistency allow different layers of the platform to connect without losing clarity.
